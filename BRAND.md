# FIELDTRACE identity usage

These SVGs are public-facing brand assets derived from the geometric FIELDTRACE mark already used in the Studio. They do not include private application logic.

| File | Mood | Intended placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/logo/fieldtrace-primary.svg` | Calm, precise, editorial | White or pale-neutral pages |
| `assets/logo/fieldtrace-night.svg` | Focused, operational, technical | Dark dashboards, night presentations |
| `assets/logo/fieldtrace-signal.svg` | Energetic, forward-looking | Launch, hero and promotional contexts |
| `assets/logo/fieldtrace-mono.svg` | Restrained, practical | Single-ink print, stamps and laser marking |
| `assets/logo/fieldtrace-app-icon.svg` | Recognizable at a glance | App tile, shortcut and square avatar |

## Optical sizes

Separate mark exports are supplied at 16, 24, 48 and 128 CSS-pixel dimensions. The source geometry is vector and can scale to other sizes. Inspect 16px rendering at device-pixel densities and adjust optical spacing before claiming final embedded readiness.

## Rules

- Keep clear space equal to at least the blue signal square on all sides of the mark.
- Use the ink plus signal-blue mark on light backgrounds and white plus blue on dark backgrounds.
- Keep the wordmark uppercase. Use a consistent modern sans family.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add bevels, apply neon glows or turn the signal square into a generic status light.
- Do not use success green or danger red as alternate brand signatures. Those colors communicate interface meaning, not brand personality.
- Verify contrast of the surrounding context. Logo color contrast is not evidence of component-text contrast.

The current wordmark uses editable SVG text and depends on available fonts. For final production assets, outline the approved wordmark from the authoritative brand typography after optical review.
